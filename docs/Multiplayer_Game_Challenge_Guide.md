# Multiplayer Game Challenge: practical guide

This is a plain-language version of the **Handshake AI Skills Studio × OpenAI Multiplayer Game Challenge** official rules. It keeps the details needed to enter and understand judging while omitting legal terms, liability clauses, dispute provisions, privacy language, and the full eligible-country appendix. Consult the original PDF for the binding rules.

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Project | Complete Handshake's **Create a Multiplayer Game** mission using OpenAI. |
| Cost | Free to enter. |
| Submission deadline | **October 30, 2026 at 11:59 PM Pacific Time.** |
| Entry location | Submit the project through the **Create a Multiplayer Game** mission in Handshake AI Skills Studio. |
| Number of entries | You may submit more than one project. |
| Prizes | The top three winners receive **$1,000 USD** prizes. |

## What to submit

Each project entry needs all four of these:

1. **Project title**
2. **Project cover image**
3. **Project description**
4. **Project link or URL**

Submit the finished project through the Handshake mission by the deadline above. The document says entrants need an active Handshake account, must be at least 18 or the age of majority, and must live in an eligible country. The full country list is in the original PDF.

## How projects are judged

Every entry is scored in four equally weighted areas. Judges use a 1–5 scale in each area.

| Criterion | Weight | What a strong entry demonstrates |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **Execution** | 25% | Meets or exceeds the mission requirements, works reliably, and is ready to demonstrate end to end. |
| **Creativity** | 25% | Has a distinctive concept, thoughtful twist, or unusually clever implementation. |
| **Usefulness / Value** | 25% | Gives its intended players a clear, compelling experience. |
| **Polish & Thoughtfulness** | 25% | Feels complete and intentional, with clear guidance, good usability, and attention to edge cases. |

### Scoring guide

- **1:** Major gaps: the project does not work reliably, lacks originality or clear appeal, or is confusing to use.
- **2:** Partial or basic result with noticeable gaps in function, differentiation, value, or refinement.
- **3:** Meets the core requirements and has a clear purpose with basic usability.
- **4:** Works well and shows originality, strong value, and thoughtful design, with only minor issues.
- **5:** Exceeds the requirements: stable, distinctive, compelling, and polished throughout.

All entries go through an initial review. The **top 20 entries** are then reviewed by a panel using the same four criteria. The top three winners are selected from that process.

## Results

Winners are notified by email at the address on their Handshake accounts within **30 days after the entry deadline**. Keep that account's email address current.
