Tandem Trails: Complete Player Guide
Play Tandem Trails
Tandem Trails is an online platform adventure for 2–4 players. Each person controls one character on their own device. Work together through 16 trails in four worlds: collect the lights, open the gate, and bring the crew to the portal. You need an internet connection, but no account or installation.
Quick start
1. One player enters a name and selects Create a Room.
2. The host shares the six-character room code.
3. Each friend enters their name and the code, then selects Join a Friend.
4. When at least two players are in the lobby, the host selects an unlocked trail on the Campaign Map and presses Start the Adventure.
5. Collect four spirit lights, activate both pressure plates with teammates, and bring all active players to the portal.
Share Invite copies a link containing the room code. The link currently opens the game without filling in the join form: the joining player should enter the code shown after room= in the link.

The on-screen Touch option changes the button order for left-handed play. On a phone, landscape orientation makes upcoming platforms easier to see.
The lobby
The lobby displays the room code and crew roster. A room holds up to four players. The host chooses the trail and starts the run; other players can see the map but cannot select the crew’s level.
Mark Ready is an optional lobby indicator. The host can start as soon as two players are present. Friends must join before the run begins.
Use Copy Code to share just the code or Share Invite to copy a link. To leave from the lobby or results screen, press Leave room twice to confirm.
How to finish each trail
1. Collect four spirit lights
Follow the glowing lights across the level. Walk or jump into each one. Every light is shared: if one teammate collects it, the whole crew gets credit. Watch the Lights count under the game screen until it reaches 4/4.
2. Open the gate together
Near the end of the trail are pressure plates A and B. Put players on both plates at the same time. The gate then opens for the crew. The stage display changes to Gate Open.
3. Gather at the portal
Move everyone who is actively playing to the portal beyond the gate. Watch the Ready count; the trail clears when all active players are there, all four lights have been collected, and the gate is open.
The first level, First Flight, shows short instructions as you reach movement, jumping, lights, plates, and the portal.
Platforms, hazards, and teamwork
- Gaps and hazards: Jump over pits and avoid thorns, gears, urchins, or storm orbs. Falling returns you to a checkpoint and increases the run’s fall count.
- Spring platforms: In Sunleaf Wilds, some platforms bounce you higher. Use them to cross wider gaps.
- Sync Bridge: Stay near a teammate to make an extra bridge solid. Bridge Live appears when the crew is close enough.
- Harmony Step: A crossing near the final gap needs both teammate proximity and the right beat. Wait for Harmony Step · Go! before jumping onto it.
- Optional fireflies: Each trail has two. Anyone can collect them for the room. They reward exploration but are not required to open the gate or finish.
Trail Twists
A shared Trail Twist changes each run:
Twist   Effect
Moon Hop    Lower gravity makes jumps longer and floatier.
Crosswind   Gusts affect you while airborne.
Quickstep   The crew moves faster.


The active twist is shown below the game screen.
What the game screen shows
The stage display helps you track the run:
- Lights: How many of the four required spirit lights the crew has collected.
- Collected: Optional fireflies found during this run.
- Timer: Your current run time.
- Sync Link: Whether a teammate is close enough to activate cooperative bridges.
- Trail Twist: The special rule for the current run.
- Gate: Whether the pressure plates have opened it.
- Ready: How many active players have reached the portal.
- Trail number: Your current level out of 16.
The crew panel shows who is in the room and who is hosting. The header shows the connection state.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                Results, medals, and replaying
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                When a trail is cleared, the results panel shows your time, falls, and medal. Faster clears earn better medals. The Campaign Map shows gold-time targets and personal bests saved in your current browser.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                You can also earn run ribbons:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                - Flawless Run: Finish without falling.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                - Lantern Keeper: Collect both optional fireflies.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                Clearing a trail unlocks the next one for the room. From the results screen, the host can select an unlocked trail on the Campaign Map and start another run.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                Sound, menu, and connection
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                Use Sound On/Off in the header for a quick mute. Menu offers separate music and sound-effect volume controls. Its secret-code field is an optional extra and is not needed to play.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                If your connection briefly drops, the header may show Reconnecting. Refreshing in the same browser normally restores your saved room seat. An away teammate will not keep a pressure plate active or prevent the active crew from clearing the portal. If the host leaves or stays away, an active teammate can become host.
